We're excited to launch the new Tupelo Amateur Radio Club website! This site is the new home for all TARC news, meeting announcements, and resources — now live at **k5tup.org**.

## What You'll Find

- **News** — Club announcements, meeting recaps, and updates right on the home page
- **About** — Learn about TARC and our club history
- **Resources** — Live band conditions with solar data, HF/VHF propagation info, repeater listings, and useful links
- **Calendar** — A live event calendar synced with our Google Calendar so you never miss a meeting or event
- **Papa Jack's** — Info on our annual Papa Jack's Trade Day (details coming as we plan this year's event)
- **Contact** — Links to our Facebook group, meeting info, and our club email

## Site Features

- **Dark mode** — Automatically matches your device preference, or toggle it manually with the moon icon in the navbar
- **Large text mode** — Hit the "Aa" button in the navbar for bigger, more readable text
- **Responsive design** — Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
- **Band conditions widget** — Live solar indices, HF band ratings, VHF conditions, and a solar corona image pulled from real-time data. Toggle between a custom view and the classic N0NBH widget. There's even a help page that explains what all the numbers mean.
