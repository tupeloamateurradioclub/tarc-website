# Papa Jack's Trade Day 2025: A Hamfest for the Record Books

**October 18, 2025 | Gateway Pavilion – Pontotoc, MS**

What an incredible day! Papa Jack's Trade Day 2025 has come and gone, and we couldn't be more thrilled with how everything turned out. This year's hamfest was our biggest ever, with an outstanding turnout of vendors, attendees, and dedicated volunteers who made the magic happen.

## A Huge Thank You to Our Vendors

We were fortunate to have some fantastic vendors join us this year, each bringing their expertise and quality products to our attendees:

- Chatt Radio
- FatCat Parts
- Efactor Antennas
- CableHelp Antennas
- And several individual vendors

A special acknowledgment goes to our friends at Chatt Radio for generously supplying our Grand Prize – an ICOM IC-7100 that had everyone excited throughout the day!

![](/images/uploads/_DSC8488.jpg)

## The Heart of the Event: Our Amazing Volunteers

While we won't list every name (we'd inevitably miss someone!), we have to recognize the incredible team effort that made this event possible. From securing and moving tables to managing the PA system, from handling the refreshment table to coordinating with local businesses – every task was covered by dedicated club members who went above and beyond.

Special mention goes to those who coordinated venue arrangements, managed ticket sales for door prizes, ensured we had proper change for transactions, captured the day through photography, and handled countless small details that might have otherwise been forgotten. One highlight that brought smiles to everyone's faces? The appearance of the Piggly Wiggly Pig, letting us all literally "Ham It Up"!

![](/images/uploads/DSC08536.jpg)

## Door Prizes Galore

Throughout the day, we gave away an impressive array of prizes, including:

- Multiple ARRL gift certificates
- Essential amateur radio reference books (Antenna Book, Repeater Directory, Towers for Hams, Parks on the Air, and more)
- High-quality PL259 connectors
- Three DX Engineering $100 gift certificates
- Various specialized guides and resources

And of course, our **Grand Prize** – that beautiful ICOM IC-7100 donated by Chatt Radio – found a very happy new home!

![](/images/uploads/_DSC8500.jpg)

## Looking Ahead

The success of Papa Jack's Trade Day 2025 has set the bar high, and we're already looking forward to next year. We'll be working on securing a date and will have announcements ready by early 2026.

To all of our attendees, vendors, and volunteers – thank you from the bottom of our hearts. This is what amateur radio is all about: community, collaboration, and sharing our passion with others.

**73, and see you next year!**
