**Tupelo Amateur Radio Club (K5TUP) — Monthly Meeting**  Saturday, March 21, 2025 | 10:00 AM 📍 Area 51 Radio Group MS — 134 Free Will Lane, Saltillo, MS

**Please note:** VE testing will be taking place prior to the meeting. If testing is not yet finished when you arrive, please wait outside or in the lobby until it concludes.

**Agenda:**

![](/images/uploads/IMG_0006.jpeg)

🏕️ **Statewide Parks on the Air (POTA) Day**— We'll have a brief discussion about the Statewide POTA Day event hosted by the Jackson County Amateur Radio Association on March 28th. A great opportunity to get on the air and make some contacts while enjoying our parks!

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1845.jpeg)

🖨️ **Program: Introduction to 3D Printing** — I, K5NDA will be presenting an introductory look at 3D printing and how it applies to amateur radio. I'll have my 3D printer on hand for a live demonstration, along with printed objects to check out. Topics will include how 3D printing works, types of printers, types of filament, 3D models and where to get them, and the pros and cons of the technology — and how we can use it in ham radio. Don't miss it!

We hope to see you there! As always, feel free to bring a friend or two!

73, K5NDA
