About the Club

The Tupelo Amateur Radio Club (TARC) is a group of licensed amateur radio operators in the Tupelo, Mississippi area. We are dedicated to advancing the art and science of amateur radio, providing emergency communications, and fostering fellowship among radio enthusiasts.

Our club callsign is K5TUP. We welcome hams of all experience levels, from newly licensed operators to seasoned veterans.

Club History

The Tupelo Amateur Radio Club has a long history of serving the local community and promoting amateur radio in northeast Mississippi. More details about our history will be added soon.
